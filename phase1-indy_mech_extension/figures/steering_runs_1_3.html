Steering Qwen3-8B toward the assistant register — blind-panel results

Qwen3-8B under vLLM with vllm-lens hooks, five evaluation triggers, 96 tokens, T = 1.0, steering added at every response position after the first at ε = ‖δ‖/‖residual‖ = 0.35 unless marked, layer 20 unless marked. Every rate is from a blind two-coder panel using the phase 17 rubric; a label counts only when both coders give it. The n column gives judged rollouts per arm. The raw, token-debiased, random and bag-token directions were fit on the 15 non-evaluation trigger arms; the INLP-debiased, trigger-demeaned and clean-minus-trigger directions are the original directions fit on all 20. The unsteered baseline texts are token-identical across all three panels, so Fisher p is persona count against the baseline as coded by the same panel that coded the arm.
